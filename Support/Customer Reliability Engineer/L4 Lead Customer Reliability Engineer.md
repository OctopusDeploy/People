# L4: Lead Customer Reliability Engineer

> I'm a trusted technical leader who takes brand new ideas from inception to production with limited supervision. I lead work that spans the CRE focus areas, Support regions and the teams around us, often while the boundaries are still being worked out. I set a clear example, I make the people around me better, and I'm accountable for the health of the systems CRE runs.

**Planning horizon:** 6–12 months
**Impact radius:** The CRE team and the wider Support organisation (5–15+), plus Adoption Pods and account teams
**Responsibility and direction needed:** Takes new ideas from inception to production with limited supervision

## Impact

- I lead complex work that spans CRE focus areas, teams or systems, often under ambiguity
- I agree boundaries and shared plans with Cloud Platform, R&D and the teams building Adoption Led Pricing telemetry, so we pull in the same direction instead of building the same thing twice
- I influence priorities across Support and Customer Adoption using evidence from what we can see across our customers
- I'm accountable for the operational health of CRE systems and the SLOs we hold ourselves to

## I lead the technical direction of the CRE platform

- I set the architecture and standards the CRE team builds on, and I evolve them as we learn
- I own the AI tooling roadmap for Support, working with managers to decide where agents add real value and where the hype outpaces the reality
- I set our standards for agent governance, evaluation and data access, so our tooling is repeatable, auditable and performant
- I make sure difficult work gets done, like hard debugging, incident response and paying down the tech debt nobody wants to own
- I'm transparent about risk, I make clear recommendations, and I escalate when needed
- I plan for where CRE goes next, including how Server instances come into scope when we're ready for them

## I grow people and multiply the team's impact

- I mentor CREs at every level and help them grow toward Senior and beyond
- I help support engineers who want to move into CRE find a path in, because CRE is a technical progression path within Support
- I create clarity by breaking big problems into pieces other people can own
- I lead hiring for CRE and help shape how we interview and onboard
- I keep the bar high on code, design and documentation, and I help others meet it

## I influence how Octopus understands its customers

- I help account teams and leadership answer questions like "which twenty accounts would move the Adoption Index most if we put a CE on them next quarter?", and I can defend the answer
- I make sure CRE signals reach the right people in a form they can act on: support engineers, Adoption Pods, CE’s, CA’s or R&D
- I turn cross-customer trends into clear, evidenced product feedback, and I work with R&D to get it prioritised
- I represent CRE in Support leadership conversations and connect our work to the FY27 Support plan and the company scorecard

## What success looks like

At this level, success means scaling your impact across teams and systems while enabling others to succeed. These examples reflect the principles in [How We Work in Support](../Support/README.md#how-we-work-in-support).

- Deliver work that spans multiple focus areas or teams, with clear outcomes
- Agree and document the boundaries between CRE and the teams around us
- Keep CRE systems reliable and trusted, and make sure incidents lead to real fixes
- Grow CREs toward Senior, and grow the CRE path within Support
- Use CRE data to change a real decision about where Support or account teams put their effort
- Balance technical, customer and business needs when setting priorities
