# L5: Principal Customer Reliability Engineer

> I'm a strategic and deeply trusted technical leader. I define how Octopus understands and improves the experience of every customer, not just the ones who raise a ticket. I'm a force multiplier: my work shapes the CRE platform, the capability of the people around me, and the decisions Octopus makes about where to put its effort.

**Planning horizon:** 12 months and beyond
**Impact radius:** Support, Customer Adoption and R&D
**Responsibility and direction needed:** Identifies problems and opportunities, builds consensus to invest in them, and drives them with minimal oversight

## Impact

- I lead large-scale initiatives that change how Support, Customer Adoption and R&D use what we know about our customers
- I shape how Customer Reliability Engineering fits into Octopus's long-term success as an adoption-led business
- I mentor senior engineers and leaders across Support and beyond
- I'm a natural escalation point for the hardest problems in CRE, and I drive them to a conclusion

## I define the approach to customer reliability at Octopus

- I own the technical vision for the CRE platform: the architecture, the data model, the AI approach and the principles we build on
- I design systems that let us understand the whole customer base, including free and Server customers, without scaling headcount to match
- I set the long-term direction for AI in Support, grounded in what we've learnt: knowledge is the foundation, the real value is in connecting systems, and AI needs constant care to stay useful
- I make sure Octopus runs its own agents on the governance it sells, and I feed what we learn back to the teams building it
- I see problems and opportunities others miss, and I have a record of acting on them in ways that last

## I shape product and strategy through customer insight at scale

- I turn aggregate customer signal into clear, evidenced opportunities that inform Octopus's product and adoption strategy
- I work with R&D and Product leadership to prioritise the reliability and adoption problems that matter most, like upgrade friction, configuration complexity and worker reliability
- I help Octopus decide where CE, SE and Support effort should go, and I make sure those decisions rest on data we can trust
- I'm a credible external voice for Octopus on CD reliability, observability and AI-assisted support, at events, in content and in conversations with customers

## I invest in the organisation and its people

- I define what a proactive, AI-assisted support organisation looks like at Octopus, and I hold a long-term view of how we get there
- I make sure CRE compounds over time, so every pattern we detect and every tool we build makes the organisation smarter, not just busier
- I develop people well beyond my own team through pairing, architecture reviews and coaching
- I influence hiring, onboarding and career development across CRE and Support
- I practise high-agency, low-ego collaboration, and I build consensus across teams to get decisions made

## What success looks like

At this level, success means setting direction that lasts and multiplying the impact of everyone around you. These examples reflect the principles in [How We Work in Support](../Support/README.md#how-we-work-in-support).

- Set a CRE direction that Support, Customer Adoption and R&D understand and back
- Deliver change that outlasts any single project
- Show that proactive signal is preventing tickets and protecting adoption across the customer base
- Grow leaders, not just engineers
- Be the person leadership asks when they need to understand how our customers are really doing
