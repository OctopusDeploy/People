# L1: Junior Customer Reliability Engineer

> I'm a curious, motivated member of the Customer Reliability Engineering team. I'm building my skills in Octopus, cloud operations, data and AI tooling, and I'm learning how to turn what we can see about our customers into something the support team can act on. I ask good questions, I take feedback well, and I'm on a clear path toward contributing independently.

**Planning horizon:** Current cycle
**Impact radius:** Self, with support from a senior CRE
**Responsibility and direction needed:** Daily to weekly guidance from a senior CRE or my manager

## Impact

- I deliver small, well-defined pieces of monitoring, data or AI tooling work with guidance
- I work mostly within the CRE team, and with support engineers when I need to understand the problem I'm solving
- I help keep what we've already built running by fixing bugs, tidying noisy alerts and keeping docs current

## I'm learning to build the tooling that makes proactive support possible

- I'm learning how Octopus works in the real world: deployment processes, projects, environments, targets, workers, runbooks, variables and Config as Code
- I contribute to our cloud instance monitoring, customer health data, antipattern detection and internal AI tooling under the guidance of a senior CRE
- I write code that's readable, tested and documented, and I ask for review early
- I use AI tools in my own day-to-day work, and I'm learning how to write, test and evaluate prompts and agents properly
- I follow our rules for how agents access customer data and systems, and I ask when I'm not sure
- I recognise when something is outside my depth and escalate promptly, with clear context

## I'm learning what a healthy customer looks like

- I'm building an understanding of what good looks like for a cloud customer: healthy workers, successful deployments, sensible configuration and a current version
- I learn from senior CREs and support engineers about the antipatterns that reliably lead to tickets, and I help build and test detection for them
- I learn from support engineers about the problems they solve every day, because they're who I'm building for
- I understand the basics of the Adoption Index and why it matters to how Octopus decides where to put effort
- I review alerts and health signals with the team, and I'm developing judgement on what needs action and what's noise

## I contribute to a kind, curious and effective team

- I avoid going dark, and I know when to call out for help
- I share what I'm learning, even when it's early or imperfect
- I take responsibility for what I produce and follow through on my commitments
- Every role has mundane tasks, and I work through them diligently
- I work with my manager and peers to build and maintain a personal growth plan

## What success looks like

At this level, success means growing confidence, reliable delivery on small pieces of work, and a real curiosity about how our customers use Octopus. These examples reflect the principles in [How We Work in Support](../Support/README.md#how-we-work-in-support).

- Ship small improvements to monitoring, data or AI tooling that a senior CRE can sign off with minimal changes
- Pick up bugs and noisy alerts on existing systems and see them through
- Explain what a tool you've worked on does, and why a support engineer or CE would care
- Build a working understanding of the four CRE focus areas and how they connect
- Ask good questions early, and act on feedback
