# L3: Senior Customer Reliability Engineer

> I'm a trusted, highly sought-after engineer with a proven record of building systems that change how Support works. I own a CRE focus area end to end, I lead by example, and I raise the bar for the quality of what the team builds. Support engineers and account teams come to me because what I build helps them do their jobs.

**Planning horizon:** Current and next cycle, up to a quarter ahead
**Impact radius:** Peers in CRE (2–5), plus the support engineers and account teams who use what we build
**Responsibility and direction needed:** Takes a focus area, or a large slice of one, from idea to production

## Impact

- I own a CRE focus area, or a large part of one, and I set the technical approach for it
- I work directly with Support managers, Adoption Pods, CA’s, CE’s and R&D to shape what we build and why
- I mentor CREs, and I help support engineers who want to move into CRE work
- I'm a calm, reliable point of escalation when our own systems misbehave

## I lead the technical work in my area

- I design systems that other CREs can build on: clear data models, sensible boundaries and alerting that people trust
- I define the signals, thresholds and SLOs for the systems I own, and I revisit them as we learn more
- I design agent workflows end to end, from the data they can reach to how we evaluate them, and I'm honest about where AI helps and where it doesn't
- I make sure our agents run on the same governance we sell to customers: scoped API keys, service accounts and audit logs that attribute agent activity back to a human
- I make build versus buy calls with evidence, like checking whether a Zendesk feature already solves the problem before we build and maintain our own
- I know AI isn't fire and forget, and I build in the feedback loops, evals and maintenance that keep it useful
- I lead the post-incident review when a CRE system fails, and I make sure what we learn turns into a fix

## I turn what we see across customers into better outcomes

- I spot patterns across tickets, instances and accounts that are hard to see one customer at a time, and I turn them into detection, KB content or product feedback
- I work with Adoption Pods and account teams so our health data answers the questions they have, in the conversations they're having
- I give R&D clear, pattern-based product feedback grounded in data
- I make sure a support engineer or CE can understand and act on every signal we send them
- I understand the difference between what we can see in Cloud and what we can't yet see in Server, and I factor that into what we build

## I shape team culture and how we work

- I lead by example: I do the unglamorous work, I write things down and I close my loops
- I help teammates level up through pairing, design reviews and honest feedback
- I help shape how we plan, review and ship work in CRE
- I take part in hiring and help new CREs get up to speed
- I'm a trusted voice in discussions about priorities and trade-offs, and I disagree constructively

## What success looks like

At this level, success means owning an area, raising the standard of what the team builds, and making the people around you better. These examples reflect the principles in [How We Work in Support](../Support/README.md#how-we-work-in-support).

- Own a focus area and show measurable impact on the people who use it
- Ship work that other CREs can confidently extend and maintain
- Keep your systems within their SLOs, and lead the review when they aren't
- Turn cross-customer patterns into detection, KB content or product fixes
- Mentor other CREs, and support engineers who are interested in CRE
- Be the person account teams and support engineers go to when they need to understand what the data is telling them
